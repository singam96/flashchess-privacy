Flash Chess – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Flash Chess is a free chess tutor for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone, and it does not request internet access: everything, including the chess engine, runs on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (lessons and puzzles done, stars, bots beaten), your play history (the games you played, so you can look at them again), and your settings (look, board, sound, vibration) are saved in small files on your own device. They never leave your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app, and "Reset progress" in Settings clears progress.

Permissions

The app asks only for the vibration permission, so the board can give a small buzz when you play. You can turn this off in Settings.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs or advertising networks, and shares no data with anyone. It contains open-source software (listed under Settings, About and licences).

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from anyone, whatever their age.
